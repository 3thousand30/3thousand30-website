privacy_and_control

Local profiles · bring your own AI key

Human profiles, saved games, board settings, and result cards stay on the PC. AI keys are protected locally; when an AI seat plays, the board request is sent only to the provider you selected.

faq

Straight answers

Does the app include AI usage?+

No. Add an AI profile with a provider and model you already use. You pay that provider directly for any AI usage.

Can I play without an AI key?+

Yes. Human versus human games do not need a provider or an API key.

Are AI moves trusted blindly?+

No. The app checks AI moves against its local chess rules before applying them to the board.
