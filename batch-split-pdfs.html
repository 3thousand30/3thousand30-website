Batch Split PDFs

Split one PDF or a whole folder into pages or fixed-size chunks locally.

Batch Split PDFs handles both the one-document job and the folder-wide job. Choose one page per output or a fixed number of pages per chunk, then follow the local batch without opening each PDF individually.
