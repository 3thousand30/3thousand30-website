BatchEnhance Image

Upscale selected images in batches for books, posters, prints, and physical products.

BatchEnhance Image helps after selection, when the strongest image is compositionally right but too small for the planned print size. It processes a folder locally and gives you consistent output settings across the set.

faq

Straight answers

Can enhancement repair every bad image?+

No. It can improve size and presentation, but it cannot rescue weak composition, severe artifacts, or missing detail.

Is it only for AI images?+

No. It can prepare ordinary photos and digital artwork as well.

Does it upload images?+

No. Processing is local.
