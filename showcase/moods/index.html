Background music for stories of every length

From love and humour to courage, wonder and peace, Moods offers instrumental music for YouTube videos, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, podcasts and other storytelling projects. The tracks were created with narration in mind; choose the mood that supports your voice and adjust the music level in your edit.

Tracks were generated with a target of approximately 90 seconds. For 15-, 30- or 60-second videos, select an excerpt and add a fade where needed. For longer videos, repeat or transition between sections after checking that the join sounds right. Actual track lengths and endings vary.
