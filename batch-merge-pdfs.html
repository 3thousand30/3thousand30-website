faq

Straight answers

How is the merge order decided?+

PDFs are merged in alphabetical filename order. Numbered prefixes such as 01, 02, and 03 make the intended order explicit.

Can it add page numbers?+

Yes. You can add fresh continuous page numbers across the merged output.

Are PDFs uploaded?+

No. The merge runs locally.
