faq

Straight answers

Which source formats are supported?+

Markdown, plain text, DOCX, JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, and GIF. Existing PDFs are skipped rather than reconverted.

Does DOCX preserve the original Word layout?+

No. It extracts paragraph text; original styling, embedded images, and page layout are not reproduced.

Can it combine a whole folder?+

Yes. Combined mode processes supported files in filename order and can add page numbers.

Does it need an AI service?+

No. PDF conversion is local and does not require an AI provider.
