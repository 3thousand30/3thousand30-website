Batch Compress PDF

Make batches of PDFs smaller on your PC without uploading them.

Batch Compress PDF reduces document sets locally. Flatten works best for most size-reduction jobs, especially scanned or image-heavy PDFs, by creating page-image copies. Smart is available when selectable text, links, forms, annotations, and PDF structure need to remain intact. Encrypted and signed files are skipped, inputs are never overwritten, and a larger candidate is never retained.

faq

Straight answers

Are my PDFs uploaded?+

No. Compression and preview rendering run locally on the Windows PC.

What is Flatten?+

Flatten renders pages into page images. It is separate because selectable text, links, forms, tags, and signatures are not retained.

Can compression make a file larger?+

The app validates candidates and keeps the original bytes whenever an optimised candidate would be larger.
