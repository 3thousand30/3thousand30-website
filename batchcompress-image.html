faq

Straight answers

Will compression always look identical?+

No. Strong compression can introduce visible artifacts, so compare representative images before processing the whole set.

Should I replace the original files?+

Keep the masters and create a separate delivery or archive copy unless you are certain the reduced files are sufficient.

Is it local?+

Yes. No image upload is required.
