faq

Straight answers

Does it include AI usage?+

No. You bring your own provider key and pay that provider directly, so you can choose the model and cost profile.

Where are outputs stored?+

Generated Markdown or text files are saved to the folder you choose on your Windows PC.

Is the whole workflow offline?+

The app and saved outputs are local. Generation requires a connection to the external AI provider you selected.
