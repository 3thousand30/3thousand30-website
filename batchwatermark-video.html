faq

Straight answers

Are source videos changed?+

No. Batch Watermark Video creates new MP4 copies in a separate output folder and leaves the source files untouched.

Can a watermark move during the video?+

Yes. Choose a horizontal, vertical, diagonal, or varied-position path. The selected placement sets the start and, where applicable, the moving row or column.

Can I add both text and a logo?+

Yes. Use either mark alone or combine text and a supported PNG, JPG, JPEG, or WebP logo.
