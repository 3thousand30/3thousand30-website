faq

Straight answers

Where does my workspace live?+

In the folder you choose. Pages, images, and attachments remain ordinary files and folders that you can open, move, or back up outside the app.

Does Personal Workspace need an account or cloud sync?+

No. It works with local workspace folders and does not require an account, cloud service, analytics, or telemetry.

How does phone transfer work?+

Create a temporary QR transfer for an unlocked page, open it from a phone on the same Wi-Fi network, and send the edited page or an attachment back to the PC.
