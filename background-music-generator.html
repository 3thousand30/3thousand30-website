BYO key · local outputs

Your chosen provider receives the music-generation request. API keys are encrypted locally for your Windows account, and generated tracks are saved to your PC rather than a 3thousand30 cloud.

faq

Straight answers

Does it include music-generation credits?+

No. You bring your own provider key, choose the model, and pay the provider directly.

Can I listen before leaving the app?+

Yes. When a provider returns a playable track, you can listen to it in the app and open the local output folder.

Where are generated tracks saved?+

Tracks are saved locally in the folder you choose. The default location is your Windows Music folder.
