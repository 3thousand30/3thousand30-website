faq

Straight answers

Does it include image-generation credits?+

No. You use your own provider key, choose the model, and pay the provider directly.

Can it keep a whole series consistent?+

Visual personas, styles, references, and reusable templates help, but you should still generate alternatives and review the set carefully.

Where are the generated images saved?+

The files are saved locally in the output folder you choose.
