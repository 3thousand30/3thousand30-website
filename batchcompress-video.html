faq

Straight answers

Are my original videos changed?+

No. Choose a separate destination folder for the compressed copies; the source files stay untouched.

Which output format should I choose?+

Choose MP4 for broad playback compatibility. Choose WebM when web-focused VP9/Opus delivery is the better fit for the destination.

Is video compression lossless?+

No. Re-encoding is lossy, so test a representative output and check the picture and audio before processing an important complete folder.
