privacy

Your work is yours. Our website analytics are optional.

Privacy is part of the product design, not a footer formality. This policy explains what the website measures, how consent works, and where app data goes.

Last updated: 7 August 2026

website_analytics

Google Analytics loads only after you accept.

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are visited, how people find the site, and which product, Microsoft Store, use-case, documentation, and external links are useful.

Before consent, the Google Analytics script is not loaded and the website sends no analytics request to Google. If you accept, the site loads measurement ID G-8N64KW0ELL, sends the current page view, and stores your choice in this browser.

Advertising storage, ad-user data, and ad-personalisation consent remain disabled. We do not add advertising pixels or retargeting trackers.

what_google_receives

Analytics data about the visit

After acceptance, Google may receive information such as the page URL, referrer, approximate location derived from network information, device/browser information, and the interaction labels we send for useful site links. Google may set analytics cookies such as _ga.

Google handles that data under its own terms and privacy practices. You can decline here and still use the complete website.

windows_apps

Local utilities stay local.

Our local utility apps process their files and settings on your Windows machine. 3thousand30 does not run a hidden file-processing cloud, require an app account, or receive telemetry from those workflows.

AI-enabled apps run locally but send the prompt or source content required for generation or translation to the external AI provider you choose and configure. API keys are stored locally using Windows protection where the product specifies it. We do not intercept or store those provider requests.

Product pages state their privacy boundary directly so “local” is not used to hide an external AI request.
