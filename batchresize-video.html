faq

Straight answers

Does resizing improve a smaller source video?+

No. A larger output frame can meet a delivery requirement, but it does not create new visual detail in smaller source footage.

Should I use Fit within or Crop to fill?+

Use Fit within when every part of the original frame must remain visible. Use Crop to fill when the target frame must be completely filled, then inspect the real-frame preview.

Are source videos changed?+

No. Batch Resize Video writes separate MP4 copies to the output folder you choose.
